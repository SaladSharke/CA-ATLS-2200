The ATLAS BTU Lab is an inclusive community makerspace facilitating projects at the intersection of Art, Design, Engineering and Architecture. It serves multiple functions as a general workspace, and workshop host.


WHO?

Anyone can gain access to BTU. Our goal is to provide the most diverse and inclusive environment for all types of makers.

HOW?

New members attend a 20 minute general orientation, covering community expectations, safety rules, and a basic overview of our equipment.

WHEN?

The orientation schedule, along with all scheduled events, can be found on the BTU Calendar.
